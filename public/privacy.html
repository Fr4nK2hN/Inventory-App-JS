Privacy Policy

Welcome to our Inventory App. Your privacy is important to us. This application operates entirely within your browser.

Data Storage & Usage

We do not use external servers to collect your personal information. We utilize your browser's localStorage to save the following data so it remains available when you return to the page:

  • Products: The inventory items you manage.
  • Categories: The custom groups you create for products.
  • Language Selection: Your preference between English and Chinese.
  • Cookie Consent: Your agreement to this policy.
